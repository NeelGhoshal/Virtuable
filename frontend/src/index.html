      What to know

      • Gestures are read by skin colour inside the box. A plain background and even light work best. If your hand is not picked up, use “Match my skin tone”.
      • Keep your face out of the box, or it can be read as a fist.
      • Palm detection can mistake other shapes for a hand. Turn it off above if your hand goes up by itself.
      • Speech uses your browser’s recogniser, which may send audio to the browser’s maker. It is available in Chrome, Edge and Safari.
      • Messages are kept only until you close this page.
